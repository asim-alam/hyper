## 10–18s — GHURNI

## 18–31s — A TRACK IS ONLY THE START.

A track shows where a cyclone may travel. But which communities face damaging winds? How many people could be exposed? And how uncertain is the answer?

Visual: map. Which communities? How many people? How uncertain?

## 31–45s — KEEP THE UNCERTAINTY.

Ghurni starts with real archived ensemble forecasts. Each line is one possible storm path. This Remal replay uses forecasts issued before landfall, rather than pretending we already knew the outcome.

Visual: tracks. Remal · archived forecast · 23 May 2024, 12:00 UTC

## 45–58s — 72. 48. 24 HOURS.

Compare forecasts around seventy-two, forty-eight, and twenty-four hours before landfall. Watch the estimated impacts change. Ghurni keeps those changes visible, rather than showing one certain answer.

Visual: cycles. Compare archived cycles. See what changed.

## 58–73s — MAKE THE IMPACT LOCAL.

Each member passes through a physics-based wind model. Local boundaries and population estimates connect winds to communities, producing wind-threshold probabilities and a range of possible population exposure.

Visual: pipeline. Physics-based wind model · population-weighted estimates

## 73–88s — 5,160 UNIONS.

Explore five thousand, one hundred and sixty unions. Search a place, inspect wind probabilities, and compare forecast cycles. These are estimates of population exposure, not casualty predictions.

Visual: capture / actual application capture. Search a place. Inspect its probability. Compare its forecast cycles.

## 88–101s — ACT. PREPARE. MONITOR.

Adjustable probability rules create Act, Prepare, and Monitor lists. The threshold stays visible, and priorities can be exported. These are planning rules for review, not evacuation instructions.

Visual: capture / actual application capture. Adjustable rules · explicit thresholds · exportable lists

## 101–114s — REVIEW BEFORE ACTION.

A template briefing combines forecasts and priorities for human review. Change the forecast or decision rule, and approval resets. Nothing is sent automatically.

Visual: capture / actual application capture. Template briefing · forecast context · approval step

## 114–127s — BANGLA + ENGLISH.

Controls, explanations, and exports work in Bangla and English. The map keeps geography central. Its dated Sentinel-two satellite mosaic provides context, not live storm imagery.

Visual: capture / actual application capture. The same map. Two interface languages.

## 127–142s — TEST. COMPARE. EXPLAIN.

Evidence compares six historical storms against a single-forecast baseline. Reference impacts use observed tracks and the same wind model. This limited backtest is not independent validation against measured damage.

Visual: capture / actual application capture. Six historical storms · three lead times · transparent limitations

## 142–154s — A PROTOTYPE. WITH LIMITS.

Ghurni is a historical wind-risk prototype. Numerical forecasts and physical modelling support preparedness analysis. Always follow official Bangladesh Meteorological Department warnings.

Visual: limits. Archived forecasts · raw probabilities · estimated exposure

Next: stronger validation and partner feedback. Flood mapping, surge, and shelter access remain planned. Help us evaluate the next steps.
