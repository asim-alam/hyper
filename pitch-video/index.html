GHURNI / ঘূর্ণি

FROM FORECAST TO ACTION.

github.com/UniqueSwagger/ghurni

Decision support only · always follow official BMD warnings

Before a cyclone reaches Bangladesh,
response teams face an urgent question.
Which communities should prepare first?
District officers and volunteers need time to brief
teams and coordinate local support.
Cyclone-prone coasts around the world face the same
gap. A track shows where the storm may
go. Local teams still have to work out
where damaging winds might reach.
Ghurni brings live monitoring and historical impact analysis
into one workspace.
Teams can check the latest reported storm position,
then use archived ensembles to explore how uncertainty
changes local priorities.
We run each archived forecast through a physical
wind model, then combine it with population data.
This turns a set of storm tracks into
local wind probabilities and estimated exposure ranges.
In Live mode,
the map combines storm fixes with model winds.
Here the feed is connected,
but the last reported fix is old.
Ghurni flags it and pauses the nowcast.
Forecast mode shows the provider’s track and uncertainty
cone when available.
Regional weather frames extend through day five.
Missing intensity stays blank.
Switch to Historical and replay Cyclone Remal.
Search for Amadi and check its wind probability.
Compare the three forecast cycles to see how
its risk changes.
Set a probability threshold to sort unions into
action tiers. Export the list or draft a
briefing for review.
Switch to Bangla for the forecast map and
source panel. Historical exports also support both languages.
A Kalman filter updates position and motion as
new fixes arrive.
Between fresh fixes, it estimates motion and tracks
uncertainty. Open Meteo supplies the separate regional weather model.
We train an isotonic regression calibrator,
leaving each test storm out of training.
Calibration did not consistently improve the model-based backtest,
so the replay displays raw ensemble probabilities.
Our proposed pilot measures time to a reviewable
priority list and whether users recognise stale fixes.
Results will guide validation and future shelter and flood layers.
